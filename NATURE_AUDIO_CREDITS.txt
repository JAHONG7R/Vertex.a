  https://www.nps.gov/subjects/sound/gallery.htm
  Direct media: https://www.nps.gov/nps-audiovideo/audiovideo/588a7add-3839-4eb9-86f1-66cfd867224a.mp3

Local WAV files included in assets/audio (rain, birds, wind, stream, fire, ocean, noise beds)
were present in the supplied Vertex audio bundle. Their provenance was not verifiable here;
they are therefore not represented as authentic field recordings.

The remote NPS tracks require an internet connection. Other unavailable sound layers
remain generated/synthetic and are labelled as such in the app.
